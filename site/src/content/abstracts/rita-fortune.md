title: Interpretations and implications of the textures and  mineralogy of microbialites in Western Victoria

An overview of the textures and mineralogy in microbialites from a maar lake in Western Victoria provide new implications for the processes occurring in ancient environments. These  microbialites, which have not been the subject of any previous studies, include textures so far undocumented in modern microbialites, but which are analogous to those found in ancient stromatolites. Dendritic textures forming from the recrystallisation of an unstable carbonate phase on short timescales challenge the prevailing view that similar textures in ancient stromatolites are created by neomorphism of aragonite during diagenesis. Additionally, the occurrence of iron sulphides alongside iron-oxide rich dendrolites hold important implications for what have been interpreted as the iron oxidising bacteria Frutexites in fossil stromatolites. A greater understanding of the biogeochemical processes occurring to produce the textures seen in these modern microbialites allows for insight into the conditions and environments of early Earth.
