After the first hour, the event will open up to the wider SGEAS community, including postgraduate students and staff. It’s a great opportunity to meet people from across academia, government, consulting and industry, and to chat about possible career pathways after graduation.

Organisations represented include CSIRO, GHD, Jacobs, WSP, SRK Consulting, the Victorian Government, the Australian Synchrotron, AIG, and several others.

There will also be free food, drinks and merch.

If you’re interested in coming, please let me know before Friday 16 October, as the organisers would like an indication of numbers.
